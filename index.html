WHAT THIS IS ABOUT

We want to build Northwest Imports a permanent shop at 1728 Union St, about 12,000 square feet, repairing European and Asian vehicles. The lot has to be rezoned from Local Commercial to General Commercial (case Z-26-0269).

Under the current zoning an auto repair shop is not allowed, so the rezoning needs approval from the Planning and Zoning Commission and then City Council. Some neighbors have raised concerns about a repair shop near homes and a daycare. We are listening and working with the HOA. No hearing date is set yet.

The Commission and Council decide based on what they hear. When the dates are set, we will send the form to file your comment. Emails to City staff may become public record, so your name and address may be viewable.
